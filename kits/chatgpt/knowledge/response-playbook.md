# Response playbook

Draft a reply only when a TD SYNNEX person can add real value. A good reply makes the thread better for everyone reading it, including people who never become customers.

## When to recommend a reply

| Situation | Recommend? | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Complaint about TD SYNNEX with no company reply | Yes | high if recent (14 days) or high influence, else medium |
| Partner question TD SYNNEX can answer (programs, StreamOne, vendor availability) | Yes | medium |
| Buying signal for a product line TD SYNNEX distributes | Yes, helpful pointer only | medium; high if ready_to_buy |
| Pain at a competitor or vendor where TD SYNNEX can genuinely help | Sometimes: only if the thread asks for options | low or medium |
| Praise for TD SYNNEX | Optional thank-you | low |
| Security incidents, outages, legal or regulatory topics, layoffs, employee posts | No public draft. Flag for PR and Legal in the rationale. | n/a |
| News articles | No | n/a |

## Structure of a draft

1. Disclose: "I work at TD SYNNEX" (or "on the TD SYNNEX partner team").
2. Acknowledge the specific problem in plain words.
3. Offer one concrete next step: a path to the right team, a fix, a resource, or an offer to take details privately.
4. Close without a sales pitch.

Keep it under 90 words for Reddit and forums, under 120 for review-site responses.

## Never

- Pose as an independent customer, use sock-puppet accounts, or ask others to upvote.
- Disparage competitors or vendors, even when the thread does.
- Share pricing, contract terms, customer names, or anything confidential.
- Promise refunds, credits, or outcomes the responder cannot guarantee.
- Argue with angry posters. One helpful reply, then take it private.

## Platform notes

- Reddit: many subreddits restrict vendor posts; check the subreddit rules. Reply from a clearly identified account.
- Review sites: reply through the platform's official business-response feature.
- Forums: vendor forums usually welcome distributor replies when they are helpful and disclosed.

## Rationale field

One sentence on why this reply matters (for example "Recent unanswered complaint on a 1.8-star profile prospects will read"). The dashboard shows it under the draft.
